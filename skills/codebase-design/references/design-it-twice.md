# Design it twice

Use only for a consequential interface with genuine uncertainty and no useful
precedent.

Produce two or three materially different interfaces. Hold outcome and
constraints constant. For each, show:

- what callers must know;
- where the seam lives;
- what complexity becomes private;
- failure and performance behavior;
- the natural test surface;
- migration cost.

Compare depth, locality, leverage, proof, and reversibility. Select one with a
concrete reason. Do not average incompatible designs or keep all variants.
