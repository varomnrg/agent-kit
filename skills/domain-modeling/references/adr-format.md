# ADR format

```markdown
# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

## Status
Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN

## Context
Facts and forces that made a decision necessary.

## Decision
The current rule, stated directly.

## Alternatives
Material alternatives and why they lost.

## Consequences
Benefits, costs, constraints, and follow-up obligations.
```

An ADR records one durable decision, not meeting minutes or a complete design.
Never mark it accepted without the owner's ruling.
